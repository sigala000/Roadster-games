How to Play Roadster Loop

Roadster Loop is a short, skill-based stunt game. Every run is a single jump: you launch an electric sports car down a night-time road, ride up a giant ramp, fly through the air, and try to land safely. A run takes about thirty seconds, and the challenge is getting a better score each time.

1. The start lights

Each run begins with four lights. They switch on red one after another, then after a short random pause the last light turns green. Press Space (or tap the screen) the moment it turns green. The faster your reaction, the faster the car reaches the ramp: ramp speed is 90 mph minus 5 mph for every second of reaction time, never below 76 mph and never above 90 mph.

If you press before the light is green you get a false start. The game beeps, shows "Too early, wait for green" and ignores your input for 0.4 seconds. A false start never launches the car, so you can simply wait and try again.

2. The road and the ramp

The car accelerates along a flat road and drives into a curved quarter-pipe ramp. It climbs a 14-unit vertical wall and leaves the top of the curl tilted slightly past vertical. You do not steer on the ramp. Your launch speed decides how high and how far the car flies.

3. In the air

Once airborne you take over. The left and right arrow keys rotate the car. The car keeps some of its spin from the ramp, so it starts to rotate on its own, and you will need to correct it. Hold Space to fire cold-gas thrusters from four ports under the floor. They push the car along its own up axis, which slows a fall when the car is upright. Thrusters burn gas from a tank, so use them in short bursts.

4. Rings

Glowing rings are placed along the flight arc. Flying through a ring gives you 500 points and refills 35% of the gas tank. Collecting rings early gives you more thruster time for the landing.

5. Landing

A landing is successful only if both conditions are true when the wheels touch down: your sink speed is 39 ft/s or less, and the car is upright within 28 degrees. Otherwise the car crashes. The HUD shows tilt and sink speed in green when they are in the safe range and red when they are not.

6. Scoring

  • Rings: 500 points each.
  • Flips: 1,500 points each time the car passes upside down.
  • Altitude: points for your maximum height.
  • Soft landing bonus: points for airtime and for touching down gently.
  • Crash: your score is halved and the grade is F.

After each run you see a grade, your score and stats such as reaction time, ramp speed, peak G-force, maximum altitude, airtime, flips, rings and touchdown speed. Your best score is saved in your browser.

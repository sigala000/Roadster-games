Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Roadster Loop is a free browser game. This page explains what data the game does and does not handle.

What the game stores

Your best score is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key roadsterLoopBest. It never leaves your device and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What we collect

The game itself does not require an account and does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It does not use analytics or tracking code.

Third-party content

The game loads the three.js library from the cdnjs content delivery network. Your browser contacts that server when the game loads, so it can see your IP address and browser details as with any web request.

If advertising is enabled on this site, ad partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can manage ad personalisation in Google Ad Settings and learn more in Google's advertising policy. Where required, a consent prompt will be shown before non-essential cookies are set.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Trademarks

Roadster Loop is an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Tesla. All car designs and branding in the game are original.

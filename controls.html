Controls & Tips

Keyboard

  • Space – start the game, launch on green, and fire thrusters in the air
  • ← – rotate the car counter-clockwise (nose up)
  • → – rotate the car clockwise (nose down)
  • Enter or Space – run it again from the results screen
  • M – mute or unmute sound

Touch screens

  • Tap anywhere to start and to launch on green.
  • Use the on-screen left and right buttons to rotate in the air.
  • Hold the thrust button to fire the thrusters.

Tips for a better score

  1. Watch the last light, not the clock. Focus on the fourth light and press as soon as it turns green. A reaction under half a second keeps you near the full 90 mph.
  2. Check your spin early. The car leaves the ramp already rotating. Use the arrow keys in the first second to decide whether you want a flip or a stable flight.
  3. Take the rings. Each ring is worth 500 points and 35% gas, so they pay for themselves.
  4. Plan your flips. Each flip is worth 1,500 points, more than three rings. Start a flip early, then use the opposite arrow to stop the spin with time to spare.
  5. Save gas for the end. Thrusters only slow your fall when the car is upright, so keep at least a third of the tank for the last few seconds.
  6. Level out before the ground. Aim to be upright with a few seconds of fall remaining, then start braking with short bursts. Watch the sink readout and keep it under 39 ft/s.
  7. Land gently, not just safely. A softer touchdown earns a bigger bonus than one just under the limit.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I press Space. Click the game once so the page has focus, then try again.
  • No sound. Browsers only allow audio after you interact with the page. Press Space or tap once, and check that you have not muted the game with M.
  • The game runs slowly. Close other heavy tabs and make sure hardware acceleration is turned on in your browser.
